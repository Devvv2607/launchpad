# About Chai & Chapter
Chai & Chapter is a bookshop café on Chapel Road, Bandra West, Mumbai, opened in 2019 by two
friends who loved second-hand books and cutting chai. Every table has a small shelf of novels
guests can read while they sit; books can be bought for ₹150-₹400.

# Menu
- Cutting chai, brewed over charcoal: ₹40
- Irani chai: ₹60
- South Indian filter coffee in a steel tumbler: ₹70
- Bun maska: ₹50; Keema pav: ₹180; Masala omelette: ₹140
- Monsoon special (June-September): masala chai with onion pakoras, ₹160

# Hours and events
Open 8am-11pm every day. Silent reading circle every Sunday at 9am (free, bring any book).
Poetry open mic on the last Friday of each month from 7pm; sign-ups at the counter.

# Policies
We don't take reservations on weekends. Free Wi-Fi, but laptops are asked to share tables after 6pm.
