# Iron & Ease
Iron & Ease is a strength-training studio in Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru, for beginners and
people returning to exercise after a break. Small groups of at most 6 people, always coached.

# Classes
- Foundations (6 weeks, Mon/Wed/Fri 7am or 7pm): learn squat, hinge, push, pull and carry.
- Strong at 40+: twice a week, joint-friendly progressions.
- Open gym hours for members: 6am-10pm.

# Pricing
Foundations course ₹9,000. Monthly membership ₹4,500 (12 classes). First trial class free.

# Coaches
Head coach Ananya Rao (NSCA-CSCS). Coaches don't give medical advice; members with injuries are
asked to get clearance from their doctor first.
