# About Kadak Tees
Kadak Tees is a Mumbai streetwear label started in 2022 by Riya and Aftab, two illustrators from Andheri. We make graphic tees about everyday Bombay life: local trains, cutting chai, monsoon chaos, Marine Drive at 2am. Every design is drawn in-house and printed in small batches.

# Audience
College students and young professionals aged 18 to 28 across Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru. They follow meme pages, thrift on Instagram, and buy tees that say something.

# Voice
Witty, warm Hinglish. Talk like a friend from the same local train: short lines, playful Bombay slang (bindaas, ekdum, kadak, scene kya hai), one or two emojis at most. Self-aware humour, never mean. Do say: drop, limited batch, drawn in Mumbai. Don't say: cheap, sale-sale-sale, body-shaming jokes, anything about other brands.

# Product facts
- Graphic tees: 100% combed cotton, 220 GSM, oversized and regular fits, sizes XS to XXL.
- Screen-printed with water-based inks so the print stays soft.
- Price: ₹699 per tee for drops; bundles of 3 for ₹1,899.
- Free shipping on orders above ₹999; cash on delivery available.
- Orders ship within 48 hours from our Andheri studio. Easy 7-day size exchange.

# Drops
We release designs in themed drops of 4 to 6 tees, announced on Instagram (@kadaktees) 3 days ahead. Each design is printed in a limited batch of 150. Past drops: "Local Train Legends" (2023), "Chai Pe Charcha" (2024), "Ganpati Bappa Morya" (2025).

# Visual identity
Primary colours: Kadak Mustard #F2B705 and Charcoal #1E1E1E, accent Rain Blue #2D6CDF, background Off-white #F4F1EA. Bold condensed type, hand-drawn illustrations, lots of texture.
