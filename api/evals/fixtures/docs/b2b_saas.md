# LedgerLoop
LedgerLoop is accounts-payable automation for Indian mid-market companies (200-2,000 employees).
It reads vendor invoices from email, matches them to purchase orders and GRNs, and pushes approved
bills to Tally Prime and Zoho Books. Founded in Bengaluru in 2022.

# Product facts
- Three-way matching (PO, GRN, invoice) with configurable tolerance rules.
- GST checks: validates GSTIN format and flags mismatched tax amounts before approval.
- Approval workflows on web and WhatsApp.
- Integrations: Tally Prime, Zoho Books, SAP Business One.
- SOC 2 Type II report available under NDA. Data stored in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1).

# Pricing
Starts at ₹40,000 per month for up to 3,000 invoices; annual billing only. Free 30-day pilot for
companies with 500+ invoices a month.
